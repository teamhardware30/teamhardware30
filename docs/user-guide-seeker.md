# Seeker Guide

## 1. Create an account
1. Open `/register`.
2. Verify the email sent to Mailpit or your SMTP inbox.
3. Sign in at `/login`.

## 2. Build your profile
1. Go to `/onboarding`.
2. Upload a PDF, DOCX, or LinkedIn ZIP.
3. Review parsed experience, education, and skills.
4. Add or remove entries and skills.
5. Enter desired titles, locations, remote preference, salary range, and job types.
6. Finish setup to reach `/matches`.

## 3. Review matches
- Open `/matches`.
- Use filters and sort controls.
- Save or hide jobs.
- Open the detail panel for score breakdown and AI explanation.

## 4. Scan a CV
1. Open a job or match.
2. Choose a resume and click **Scan my CV**.
3. Review checklist, keyword hits/gaps, and bullet rewrites.
4. Use **Create tailored copy** to generate an ATS-safe PDF.

## 5. Apply
1. Open a job with a match score of 50 or higher.
2. Click **Apply**.
3. Confirm consent in the modal.
4. Track the application in `/applications`.

## 6. Manage data
- Export account data from `/account/export`.
- Delete the account from `/account/delete`.
- Download or remove resumes from `/profile`.
